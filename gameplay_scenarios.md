# Gameplay Concepts: How They Play Out

Here is a detailed look at how the **"False Multiplayer"** (Ghost) and **"Impossible Door"** mechanics would unfold in-game.

---

## 2. The Ghost ("False Multiplayer")
*Concept: Using recorded player data to create hallucinations of other subjects.*

### **The Encounter**
You are walking down the corridor. It has been empty for 10 minutes.
Suddenly, you see a silhouette, **100 units ahead**. It’s not static—it’s moving. It walks hesitantly, stops, checks a corner, and then starts running.

**"WAIT!"** you assume it's another player (or an NPC).
You sprint to catch up. The figure doesn't acknowledge you. It seems terrified of something *you can't see*.

### **The Twist**
As you get closer, the figure does something specific—it **trips** or **looks back** violently.
You realize:
1.  **It’s You**: The figure is wearing your clothes.
2.  **It’s a Loop**: The figure is re-enacting movements you made 5 minutes ago (or in a previous failed run).
3.  **The Death**: The figure suddenly screams (silent or muffled) and is "dragged" into a wall or simply vanishes into the floor.

### **The Aftermath**
You are now standing exactly where the figure vanished. The realization hits: **You are following a path that leads to death.** The game has used your own desire for connection/company to lure you into a trap.

## 3. The Impossible Door (Non-Euclidean Geometry)
*Concept: Seamless transitions to unconnected internal spaces.*

### **The Discovery**
The corridor is endless concrete. But then, standing in the exact center of the hall, is a pristine, oak-wood **Doorframe**.
It has no wall attached. It stands free-standing on the concrete floor.

*   **Walking Around It**: If you walk past it, you just see the back of the wooden frame and more corridor. It’s just an object.
*   **Looking Through It**: If you stand in front and look *through* the open door, you don’t see the corridor. You see a **childhood bedroom** or a **hospital room**. The lighting is different (warm, incandescent).

### **The Transition**
You step through the frame.
*   **Instant Shift**: There is no loading screen. As your camera passes the threshold, the "Corridor" logic cuts. You are now inside the bedroom.
*   **Audio Shift**: The hum of the facility stops. It's dead silent, or you hear a ticking clock.

### **The Trap**
You turn around to step back out.
**The door is gone.**
The bedroom is now a solid box. There is no exit.
*   **Geometric Decay**: As you panic, the bedroom stretches. The bed elongates. The window shows the *Corridor* outside instead of a street.
*   **The Escape**: To leave, you must perform a specific "Logic" (e.g., turn the light switch off). When the lights go out, the walls dissolve, and you are back in the Corridor—but miles deeper than before.
