# Taking Facility Horror to the Next Level

Based on the current foundation of paranoia, liminal spaces, and the "Facility OS" meta-narrative, here are concepts to deepen the experience.

## 1. **Fourth Wall & Hardware Immersion**
*   **Microphone Input (Breath Detection)**:
    *   Use the Web Audio API to detect microphone volume.
    *   *Mechanic*: If the player breathes too loudly or speaks, the "Paranoia" level spikes, or the entity behind them gets closer. You have to *physically* hold your breath during tense moments.
*   **Webcam "Reflection"**:
    *   Rarely, surfaces (mirrors, puddles, or black screens) could flash a processed, ghostly feed of the *actual player's face* using the webcam (with permission), blending reality with the game.

## 2. **Advanced "OS" Narrative**
*   **The OS Glitch / "Jailbreak"**:
    *   The `FACILITY_OS` currently monitors you. A "next level" step would be a second voice—a rogue glitch or previous subject—hacking the UI.
    *   *Implementation*: Messages that appear in different colors (Green vs Red) or text that overwrites the OS instructions (e.g., "DON'T LOOK BACK" becomes "D̶O̶N̶'̶T̶ LOOK B̶A̶C̶K̶" -> "LOOK BACK").
*   **Fake System Crashes**:
    *   Simulate a browser crash or a Windows/Mac error screen that is actually part of the game. When the "system" reboots, the facility has changed (e.g., upside down, textureless).

## 3. **Non-Euclidean & Impossible Geometry**
*   **The Impossible Door**:
    *   A door frame in the middle of the corridor. Walking *around* it shows nothing. Walking *through* it leads to a completely different biome (e.g., a forest, a bedroom) that seamlessly transitions back to the corridor after a few steps.
*   **Scale Shift (The "Alice" Effect)**:
    *   Slowly change the player's scale or the corridor's scale over time without them noticing. Suddenly, the floor tiles are massive, or the ceiling is miles high, inducing vertigo.

## 4. **"False" Multiplayer (The Ghost)**
*   **Recorded Data**:
    *   Record the movement paths of previous runs (or other players via a database).
    *   *Mechanic*: You see a shadowy figure in the distance. It's not an AI monster; it's a recording of *you* from 5 minutes ago (or a previous death). Just watching "yourself" run blindly into the void is terrifying.

## 5. **Bio-Feedback & Rhythm**
*   **Heartbeat Sync**:
    *   The camera bob and sound design align with a simulated heart rate. Running faster increases the BPM.
    *   *Mechanic*: If your heart rate gets too high (in-game), you suffer a "Panic Attack" (blurred vision, loss of control) and must stand still to calm down... but standing still risks the Drown Ending. A constant tug-of-war.

## 6. **Dynamic "Sanity" Assets**
*   **Asset Corruption**:
    *   As Paranoia increases, textures shouldn't just warp; they should degrade.
    *   Normal concrete walls start showing faces.
    *   Audio files play backwards or at 0.5x speed.
    *   The `FACILITY_OS` UI starts typo-ing or speaking in gibberish/binary.
