# System Narrative & Ending Analysis

## **Narrative Overview**
The *Facility* is an infinite, liminal space—a corridor that stretches forever. You play as a subject navigating this space, monitored by an Operating System (`FACILITY_OS`). The core conflict determines your mental state: **Paranoia**.

The narrative drives you through fear and uncertainty. The system leaves messages on the walls, seemingly helpful but often contradictory or taunting ("DON'T LOOK BACK", "KEEP GOING").

### **Core Mechanics & Themes**
*   **"DON'T LOOK BACK"**: The primary rule. Looking back causes paranoia spikes, screen shakes, and aggressive auditory hallucinations ("turn around" triggers).
*   **The Run (Psychosis)**: Running forward continuously increases paranoia. High paranoia leads to a "Psychosis" state, where reality breaks, lights flicker, and the environment drifts.
*   **The Stillness (Apathy)**: Standing still calms your paranoia, but if you linger too long, you succumb to **Apathy**. The facility rejects your inaction, leading to the **Drown Ending**.

## **The Endings**
The game features three standard endings, each associated with a specific keyword and action. These keywords accumulate in your "Ending History" to unlock the *True Ending*.

### **1. THE LOOP ("BACK")**
> *"Remorse is the poison of life."*

*   **Trigger**: Enter the **Mirage Room** (a dark, looped version of the intro) and walk through it to return to the beginning of the corridor.
*   **Meaning**: Represents **Regret** and the inability to move on. By circling back, you trap yourself in the past.
*   **Visuals**: The world loops seamlessly, but the message "BACK" appears briefly.

### **2. THE DROWN ("LOOK")**
> *"Man is the only creature who refuses to be what he is."*

*   **Trigger**: Stand still for too long (approx. 60s). Even as the `FACILITY_OS` warns you with bells and whispers, you refuse to move.
*   **Meaning**: Represents **Denial** and **Apathy**. In your refusal to participate in the "rat race" of the corridor, the facility is flooded by a surreal ocean. You "look" at the truth—or perhaps nature reclaiming the artificial space—and are consumed by it.
*   **Visuals**: Water rises from the floor, the walls drift apart, and a distant sun appears before you fade to black.

### **3. THE VOID ("DON'T")**
> *"He who fears suffering is already suffering from what he fears."*

*   **Trigger**: Run forward blindly while in a state of Psychosis. You must traverse a vast distance (~200 units) while highly paranoid to break out of the facility's geometry and reach the **Black Hole**.
*   **Meaning**: Represents **Fear** and **Avoidance**. You ran so hard from the "monster" behind you that you ran straight into the end of existence. By obeying the rule "DON'T LOOK BACK" to its extreme, you find only oblivion.
*   **Visuals**: The corridor disintegrates into a tunnel of stars, leading to a massive Black Hole that consumes you.

---

## **The True Ending**
If you achieve 3 endings in a single session (or sequential reloads), the **Ending Tracker** evaluates your "History".

*   **Combination**: The game analyzes your choices (e.g., `DON'T - DON'T - LOOK`).
*   **Result**: A unique philosophical quote is displayed, reflecting the specific combination of Fear, Regret, and Denial you demonstrated.
*   **Ultimate Goal**: There is no "escape." The True Ending is simply a moment of clarity—a quote from a philosopher (Nietzsche, Montaigne, Seneca) that contextualizes your suffering before the history is wiped and the simulation resets.
